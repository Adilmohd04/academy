# Student Portal Redesign - "Calm & Premium" Theme

## Overview
The Student Portal has been redesigned to match the "Modern Digital Madrasa" aesthetic:
- **Background**: Warm off-white / Sand (`#F9F7F2`).
- **Text**: Deep Charcoal / Slate (`text-slate-800`) for better readability.
- **Sidebar**: Warm Stone (`#EFECE6`) with Slate text and White active states.
- **Accents**: Muted Gold (`text-amber-700`) and Soft Olive (`text-emerald-700`).
- **Cards**: White with soft Stone borders (`border-stone-200`) and subtle shadows.

## Modified Files
1. `frontend/app/student/layout.tsx`
   - Updated background color.
   - Updated pattern opacity and color.
2. `frontend/components/ui/StudentSidebar.tsx`
   - Complete color overhaul (Stone/Slate theme).
   - Updated logo and icon colors.
3. `frontend/app/student/StudentDashboardClient.tsx`
   - Updated stats cards to be softer.
   - Updated typography to Slate/Charcoal.
   - Removed harsh gradients.
4. `frontend/app/student/meetings/MeetingsPageClient.tsx`
   - Updated meeting cards and buttons.
   - Consistent "Calm" styling.

## Visual Identity
- **Smooth**: No harsh contrasts.
- **Warm**: Sand/Stone tones instead of cold white/grey.
- **Focus**: Distraction-free typography.
