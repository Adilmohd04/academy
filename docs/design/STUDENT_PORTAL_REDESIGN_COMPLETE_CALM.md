# Student Portal Redesign Complete - "Calm & Premium" Theme

## Status: Completed
The Student Portal has been fully redesigned to match the "Modern Digital Madrasa" aesthetic requested.

## Key Changes Implemented

### 1. Visual Identity
- **Background**: Warm off-white / Sand (`#F9F7F2`) - Replaced cold white/grey.
- **Typography**: Deep Charcoal / Slate (`text-slate-800`) - Replaced harsh black/emerald.
- **Accents**: Muted Gold (`text-amber-700`) & Soft Olive (`text-emerald-700`).
- **Texture**: Subtle Islamic geometric pattern in dark grey/olive (opacity 2-3%).

### 2. Component Updates
- **Sidebar (`StudentSidebar.tsx`)**:
  - Background: Warm Stone (`#EFECE6`).
  - Active State: White card with soft shadow.
  - Inactive State: Slate text, hover effect.
  - Logo: Dark text with muted gold icon.

- **Dashboard (`StudentDashboardClient.tsx`)**:
  - Stats Cards: White with soft Stone borders (`border-stone-200`).
  - Hadith Card: Parchment style (`bg-[#EFECE6]`) with dark text.
  - Upcoming Sessions: Clean white cards, no harsh gradients.

- **Meetings (`MeetingsPageClient.tsx`)**:
  - Session Cards: Clean, distraction-free design.
  - Buttons: Slate-800 (Charcoal) primary buttons.
  - Tags: Stone/Slate neutral tags.

- **Resources (`StudentResourcesPage`)**:
  - Resource Cards: Stone-50 backgrounds.
  - Icons: Muted Amber/Emerald.

- **Profile (`StudentProfilePage`)**:
  - Profile Card: Clean white with Stone borders.
  - Form Inputs: Soft borders, Slate text.
  - Header: Slate-800 background.

## Verification
- All student-facing pages now share a consistent "Calm" theme.
- Eye strain reduced by removing high-contrast white/black.
- "Smooth like butter" aesthetic achieved through soft colors and rounded corners.
